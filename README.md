## Overview

 

> *Interstellar* is a 2014 science fiction film directed by **Christopher Nolan**.  
> The story follows a group of astronauts who travel through a wormhole near Saturn  
> in search of a new home for humanity.

 
---


## Why I Love It

 

- It's **visually stunning**
- It blends science with **emotion and humanity**
- The soundtrack by *Hans Zimmer* is incredible
- Makes me think deeply about **time, space, and love**
